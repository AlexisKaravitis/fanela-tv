# Fanela privacy policy

*Last updated: 11 November 2026*

Fanela has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't collect personal information.

**What stays on your TV:**
- the clubs you follow and your favourite club
- your language, country and other settings
- saved fixtures and images

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling Fanela deletes them.

**What goes over the internet:** to show fixtures, scores and channels, Fanela downloads public sports data and
TV guides from:
- ESPN
- EuroLeague
- TheSportsDB
- Sport24
- MagentaTV
- SofaScore

The lists of TV partners per country are built into the app, so choosing a country sends nothing anywhere.

Like any web request, these services can see your TV's IP address. Fanela doesn't send them anything about you,
and it doesn't use your location. The country is one you choose in Settings, starting from the TV's own setting.

**Home-screen rows (Android TV only):** if you turn them on, Fanela gives the TV's home screen pictures of match
cards to display. They stay on the TV.

**Children:** Fanela is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be in the app (Settings > Privacy) and on this page.
