# Fanela privacy policy

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

Fanela has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't collect personal information.

**What stays on your TV:**
- the clubs you follow
- your language and other settings
- saved fixtures and images

Clearing the app's data or uninstalling Fanela deletes them.

**What goes over the internet:** to show fixtures, scores and channels, Fanela downloads public sports data and TV
guides from:
- ESPN
- EuroLeague
- TheSportsDB
- Sport24
- MagentaTV
- SofaScore

To check for app updates, it also asks GitHub whether a newer version exists.

Like any web request, these services can see your TV's IP address. Fanela doesn't send them anything about you, and it
doesn't use your location.

**Home-screen rows (Android TV only):** if you turn them on, Fanela gives the TV's home screen pictures of match cards
to display. They stay on the TV.

**Children:** Fanela is a general-audience app and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the new version will be in the app (Settings > Privacy) and on this page.
